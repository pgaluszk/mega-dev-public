How the counts are made

  • The export pages overlap, so the same report can appear twice. One id is one report.
  • The cause comes from the tenant (locale, booking form) and the channel, as in causes.ts. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and is not used.
  • The desk’s one-day-move reads come out of the group they were filed in.
