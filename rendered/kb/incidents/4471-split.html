  • Parser. The shared parser reads dates month first; every tenant on file writes the day first. Any channel, every tenant.
  • Form hint. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type month first, and the form reads the tenant’s way. Only through the form, only on booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it.
  • One-day move. Not a swap: the date moves by one day, and depends on where the reader is (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). The desk already noted these reports (desk-notes.json); causes.ts still files them under the parser by tenant.

How the counts are made

  • The export pages overlap, so the same report can appear twice. One id is one report.
  • The cause comes from the tenant (locale, booking form) and the channel, as in causes.ts. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and is not used.
  • Each report is counted once, where the desk files it. The desk’s one-day-move reads stay in the parser count and also get their own row.
